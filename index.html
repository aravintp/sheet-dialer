Connect your Google Sheet

Turn on sheet updates (one time) With this on, the app reads your tabs and the Status dropdown, and every status you tap is written straight into the sheet. Without it, paste only the Google Sheet link: the app can read a sheet shared as "Anyone with the link", but statuses stay on this device.
  1. In your sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete what's there, paste the script below, and click Save.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment, choose type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy and allow access.
  4. Copy the Web app URL and paste it in the first box above.
Keep that link private. Anyone who has it can read and change the sheet.
